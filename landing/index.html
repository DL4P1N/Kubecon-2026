What the app stores

  • The sign-up chat says what registering does before it asks anything. Your profile is kept, so your next conference recommends talks from the first minute.
  • Organizers see aggregate interest counts, never individuals, and use them to choose future talks. Groups under 5 are folded into other.
  • Room tokens are signed, short-lived and carry only an attendee ID. The server checks entitlement on each scan; a scan logs only the decision and room.
  • The collector forwards an allowlist of attributes, never a blocklist. A test asserts that no attendee ID or location appears in exported spans.
  • There are no questions about health, politics or identity.
  • See live API status

Questions

Do I need to give my email?

No. Registration needs a name and one topic. Email is optional and not stored.

Does the app track where I am?

No. Room check-in is a QR scan at the door. There is no raw location and no per-device tracking.

What happens to my data after the event?

Your profile is kept, so your next conference recommends talks from the first minute and you answer the questions only once.

How do organizers use it for future talks?

They see aggregate counts, never individuals, with groups under 5 folded into other. The counts help choose future talks.

Is the cluster map proven?

No. The minimum group size is untested on live attendee data, and with 170 to 200 attendees the map may look sparse.
