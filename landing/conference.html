After you register

03 / 04

The chat says what registering does before it asks anything. You answer once, and your next conference starts from your profile.

  1. ChatA host asks a few questions, the first time only.
  2. ScheduleYour answers become talks with no overlaps.
  3. DoorsA short-lived QR code opens the rooms you are cleared for.
  4. Next timeYour profile is kept, so the next conference recommends talks from the first minute.
  5. Interest mapYour interests count in aggregate only. Groups under 5 fold into other, and organisers use the counts to choose future talks.
